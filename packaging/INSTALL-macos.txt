motion-blur
===========

This archive contains blurcli only. The OBS plugin has never been built or
tested on macOS, and shipping an untried binary is worse than shipping none.

blurcli
-------
Copy `blurcli` anywhere on your PATH.

Container files (--input/--output) need `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` on your PATH;
blurcli spawns them rather than linking them. Raw pipe mode (--raw) needs
nothing at all.

    blurcli --input capture240.mp4 --output out.mp4 --frames 4
    blurcli --input gameplay60.mp4 --output out.mp4 --interpolate 8 --frames 8

Run `blurcli --help` for the full option list.

The binary is arm64. On an Intel Mac, build from source - the engine has no
dependencies and `cmake -S . -B build && cmake --build build` is the whole
procedure.
